## What's Included in This Service?

We install driveway gates, perimeter fencing, and exterior finishes that secure your property and elevate its curb appeal.

### This service includes:

* Free site measurement and layout
* Gate hardware with manual or automated operable systems
* Fencing installed to local setback and height codes
* Post setting with proper footing and anchoring
* Finishing, sealing, and protective coating
* Complete cleanup of the work area

## Work Process

1. **Measurement** — We assess the entry points and set the layout (1 day)
2. **Material Selection** — Gate style, hardware, and finish selection (2–3 days)
3. **Installation** — Setting posts, hardware, and gate operation (2–5 days)
4. **Adjustment** — Fine-tune alignment and operation, then handoff

## Who Is This Service Ideal For?

Property owners in North Carolina who want a secure, attractive entrance and defined boundary that complements the architecture of their home or business.
