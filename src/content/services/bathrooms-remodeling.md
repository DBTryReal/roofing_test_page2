## What's Included in This Service?

We transform dated bathrooms into modern, functional spaces with proper waterproofing, quality tile work, and fixtures chosen to match your style and budget.

### This service includes:

* Free design consultation and layout
* Demolition and disposal of existing finishes
* Waterproofing of wet areas to standard
* Tile, drywall, and finish installation
* Fixtures, plumbing, and lighting rough-in
* Complete cleanup of the work area

## Work Process

1. **Consultation** — We assess the space and discuss your goals (1 day)
2. **Permit & Materials** — Permitting if required, and material selection (3–7 days)
3. **Rough-In & Waterproofing** — Plumbing, electrical, and moisture barriers (2–4 days)
4. **Finish & Handoff** — Tile, fixtures, and final walkthrough

## Who Is This Service Ideal For?

Homeowners in North Carolina who want a modern, updated bathroom that adds comfort, safety, and resale value, with minimal disruption to daily life.
