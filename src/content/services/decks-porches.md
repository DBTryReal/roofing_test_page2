## What's Included in This Service?

We design and build custom decks, screen porches, and covered patios engineered to withstand the humidity, wind, and rain of the southeastern United States.

### This service includes:

* Free on-site design consultation
* Custom engineering for proper drainage and load
* Premium, weather-resistant framing (treated or composite lumber)
* Railing systems built to local building codes
* Optional roofing, screening, and lighting
* Complete cleanup of the work area

## Work Process

1. **Consultation** — We visit your property, capture measurements, and discuss your vision (1 day)
2. **Design & Materials** — Layout plans and material selection for your climate (3–5 days)
3. **Construction** — Full build with inspections where required (3–10 days depending on size)
4. **Walkthrough** — Final review with you before handoff

## Who Is This Service Ideal For?

Homeowners in North Carolina who want to extend their living space outdoors with a durable, code-compliant deck or porch that adds lasting value to the property.
