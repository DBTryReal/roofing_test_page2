## What's Included in This Service?

We install premium flooring systems — hardwood, tile, laminate, and concrete — across residential and commercial spaces, prepared for the local climate.

### This service includes:

* Free measurement and subfloor inspection
* Subfloor preparation and leveling
* Moisture-barrier installation where required
* Flooring installation with expansion joints and transitions
* Trim, baseboards, and detail finishing
* Complete cleanup of the work area

## Work Process

1. **Measurement** — We assess the subfloor and capture dimensions (1 day)
2. **Material Selection** — Product selection and acclimation of materials (3–5 days)
3. **Installation** — Full install with transitions and trims (2–6 days depending on area)
4. **Handoff** — Curing time, walkthrough, and care instructions

## Who Is This Service Ideal For?

Homeowners and business owners in North Carolina who want durable, attractive flooring that matches the existing architecture and resists the local humidity.
