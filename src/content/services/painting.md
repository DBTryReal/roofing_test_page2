## What's Included in This Service?

We deliver clean, long-lasting results on interior and exterior surfaces, with proper surface preparation and premium materials chosen for durability.

### This service includes:

* Free color and finish consultation
* Surface cleaning, scraping, and sanding
* Patching, caulking, and priming
* Two coats of premium paint (interior or exterior)
* Care of surrounding surfaces and cleanup
* Complete debris removal at handoff

## Work Process

1. **Consultation** — We assess the surfaces and discuss colors (1 day)
2. **Prep** — Cleaning, patching, priming, and protection of adjacent surfaces (1–3 days)
3. **Application** — Two coats with premium paint (2–4 days depending on area)
4. **Clean & Handoff** — Remove protection, final walkthrough

## Who Is This Service Ideal For?

Homeowners and business owners in North Carolina who want a fresh, durable finish that protects and enhances the look of their property, with minimal disruption.
